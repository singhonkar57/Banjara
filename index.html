Our Story

Born from a love for honest street food.

Banjara started as a humble corner in Sector 13, Karnal — a place where flavor came first and short-cuts were never on the menu. Today, we serve thousands of happy customers every week.

Every Kathi roll is hand-stuffed with marinated, freshly cooked fillings. Every momo is folded by hand and steamed or fried to order. We make our signature sauces in-house, daily.
